### General slide feedback

- Faster through the slides
    - Not by speaking faster
    - By saying less
- You can assume that if you say something, they will believe you
    - No need to tell a whole story for every single bullet point on your slide just to arrive at that conclusion.
    - You can be very explicit.
- Instead of one slide with quite a few bullet points, try splitting it into multiple slides
    - This makes it easier to manage your timing
- Overview slides are not really necessary (e.g., I had a slide with all my RQs)
    - Just mention which RQ you are going to discuss before diving into it
    - For example, use a chapter slide with a short summary of your RQ
        - Does not need to be exhaustive
- For evaluation points, think of it like running into your supervisors on the street and they ask you how your end result turned out
    - Focus on the three things you would mention in that situation
- The title of your plot slides should be the key takeaway from the slide, not just a description of what is on it
- Make backup slides for any you had to cut, and include figures you did not use in your thesis presentation
    - Already prepared this :)

### Example questions

- Future Work
    - How would you implement...?
    - How would you adapt...? If you were to do so, how would you proceed?
- Conclusion
    - Is this a good direction?
    - How would you proceed from here?
- Results
    - How did you draw those conclusions?
    - What evidence supports your findings?
- Unaddressed Topics
    - Any aspects they would like to discuss that were not covered in your presentation
- Probing for knowledge
    - How much more do you know beyond the bare minimum?  
        -> Part of the questions will be about your content and work, but some questions may go beyond that.
    - Has the student gained additional knowledge; have they done more than just what was asked of them?  
        -> You cannot really prepare for this in detail.  
        -> All your research from the past year is essentially your preparation.
- Some students used ChatGPT to generate their Future Works, so these often get caught out by these questions
